# CPG Cranberry — Synthetic End-User Profiles (Step 1 / DE Steps 3 & 5) — v2

> **HUMAN VALIDATION MANDATE:** These profiles are synthetic, AI-generated constructs used as an exploratory sandbox to refine hypotheses, interview questions, and strategic frameworks. They cannot reliably predict real human purchasing behavior, taste preferences, or willingness-to-pay. Every finding derived from them MUST be validated through real-world human interviews and sales tests.

**Revision note (v2):** Re-centered on founder feedback: *"our target client is like a girl who shops at Happier Grocery and wants a cute chic snack — think like a lux product."* Nine of ten profiles are now aesthetic-driven women in the luxury-grocery orbit. Skeptics are drawn from **inside** that world (not mass-market outsiders), so objections reflect the real beachhead. Daniel Chen is retained from Step 5 as the partner / household economic buyer and gift purchaser (feeds Step 12 DMU).

## Beachhead constraints (Step 3 End User Profile — applied to all 10)
- Primarily women, 25–41, living in or near luxury-grocery metros (NYC core; LA, Miami, Austin secondary)
- Individual income $110k–$140k (Maya Chen band) or household income $140k–$190k (Daniel Chen band)
- Shop at least monthly at luxury/specialty grocers: **Happier Grocery**, Erewhon, Foxtrot, Eataly, Whole Foods, Citarella, Dean-&-DeLuca-style boutiques
- Treat snacks as part of personal aesthetic and self-care; discover via TikTok/Instagram/newsletters
- Buy for themselves (primary) and as gifts/hostess items (secondary)

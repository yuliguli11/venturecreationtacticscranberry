CPG Cranberry · Synthetic Primary Research · n = 10

Research Dashboard

Ten synthetic interviews with luxury-grocery shoppers (Happier Grocery, Erewhon, Whole Foods orbit), spanning innovators to skeptics, scored against the four Step 20 key assumptions for an elevated, everyday cranberry snack.

Human Validation Mandate. Every respondent here is an AI-generated persona. This dashboard is an exploratory sandbox for refining hypotheses, interview questions and strategy. It cannot reliably predict real purchasing behavior, taste preferences or willingness-to-pay. Synthetic answers were also unusually uniform (10 of 10 said "fancy Craisins"), which likely overstates consensus. Validate every finding with real interviews and sales tests before acting.

**Daniel:** I don't really have a strong feeling either way. Sour Patch Kids as a kid, sure. Some of the dried fruit is tart, mango's more sweet. I wouldn't seek it out, I wouldn't avoid it. My wife likes sour stuff more than I do, she's into those yuzu things.

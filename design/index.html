What's inside

Three ingredients, and where they come from.

Most dried cranberries are sliced, soaked in syrup and dried fast. Ours arrive whole from organic family bogs, are lightly infused with organic cane sugar, and dry slowly at low temperature, so the fruit keeps its shape, its chew and most of its tartness.

We add a touch of sunflower oil so the berries don't clump in the pouch. That's the full list for The Original. The other flavors add one thing each: dark chocolate, blood orange peel, or Sicilian pistachio.

We don't make health claims. It's a snack you eat on purpose, and we'd like you to know exactly what's in it.
